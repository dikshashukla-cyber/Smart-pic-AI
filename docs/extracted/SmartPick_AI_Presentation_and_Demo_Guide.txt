1. Suggested slides
    Slide 1 — Title: SmartPick AI, author, institution, guide.
    Slide 2 — Problem statement and target users.
    Slide 3 — Dataset source, features, and data-cleaning steps.
    Slide 4 — Architecture and recommendation method.
    Slide 5 — Live demo: select budget/preferences, show Top 5 and comparison.
    Slide 6 — Evaluation, test results, and limitations.
    Slide 7 — Conclusion and future work.
 
2. Demo script
    Start with a clear user profile: budget, main use, and two priorities.
    Generate recommendations and explain why the top result ranks highly.
    Change one preference and show how ranking changes.
    Use comparison view to compare two eligible phones.
    Finish by explaining one limitation and one future improvement.
 
3. Viva prompts
    What is a recommender system?
    How is content-based filtering different from collaborative filtering?
    Why are features scaled?
    What does cosine similarity measure?
    Why is budget a hard constraint?
    What data is needed to evaluate Precision@K or NDCG@K?
    Why is a weighted score not necessarily a trained ML model?
    What are the main limitations of the dataset?
 
4. Honest reporting
    Show only metrics actually computed from the documented test set.
    Separate implemented features from planned features.
    Do not claim live prices, real users, or validated accuracy without evidence.
